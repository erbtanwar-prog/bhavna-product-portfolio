Capstone · Travel technology · Conversational AI

MakeMyTrip AI Trip Assistant

Design a conversational trip-planning assistant that collects the traveller’s constraints and creates a useful day-by-day itinerary—with relevant stays and activities—while reducing bias toward popular, expensive, or geographically concentrated listings.

User researchPersonasCompetitive analysisPRDPrototypingAI evaluation

01 · Problem & discovery

Combined primary survey research with secondary research and competitor review to understand planning friction, decision confidence, and the information travellers need before comparing options.

02 · User needs

Developed two distinct proto-personas: travellers seeking a quick, end-to-end plan and comparison-driven travellers who want control over trade-offs and choices.

03 · Product direction

  • Collect destination, dates, trip duration, budget, and traveller needs.
  • Generate a structured day-by-day plan with relevant stays and activities.
  • Make assumptions visible and allow users to refine constraints.

04 · Quality & trust

Considered evaluation for relevance, constraint adherence, itinerary coherence, diversity of recommendations, and clear handling of missing information or uncertainty.

What this demonstrates: research synthesis, persona development, problem definition, product requirements, AI product judgement, and responsible evaluation. This is an academic capstone prototype, not an official MakeMyTrip product. Open prototype ↗
Explore case study: Nova — AI Study Assistant for UPSC Aspirants
EdTech · Applied AI · UPSC preparation

Turn scattered study material into structured learning workflows

UPSC aspirants work with large volumes of notes, PDFs, articles, and other sources. Nova’s intended product direction is to help them consolidate material, understand difficult concepts, revise efficiently, practise exam-oriented questions, and organise answer preparation.

Problem framingMVP definitionAI reliabilityLearning efficiency

01 · Target users & problem

UPSC CSE aspirants preparing for Prelims and Mains, especially learners managing multiple sources and needing structured, exam-relevant revision.

02 · Intended core workflows

  • Consolidate supplied notes, PDFs, and articles into structured revision notes.
  • Explain difficult concepts with simple language, examples, and analogies.
  • Generate UPSC-style practice questions and support self-assessment.
  • Extract key facts, keywords, comparisons, and mind-map structures.
  • Help organise arguments and answer structures for exam-oriented writing.

03 · Reliability & trust

Design for source-grounded responses, explicit uncertainty, and verification of important factual claims against reliable sources. Key risks include hallucinations, outdated facts, poor source quality, privacy, copyright, processing limits, latency, and AI API costs.

04 · Success measures to track

  • Task completion across core study workflows.
  • Usefulness ratings for generated outputs.
  • Factual accuracy in sampled responses.
  • Repeat usage and weekly active users.
  • Time to turn source material into revision-ready notes.
